US import and tariff news for robotics buyers

Short briefings on the tariffs, exclusions and import bans that change what robotics components and data-collection devices cost to land in the US. Updated 29 Oct 2026.

  • 29 Oct 2026Import ban · UFLPAThe UFLPA import-ban list reaches 190 companiesGoods made by companies on the UFLPA Entity List are presumed to involve forced labor and are barred from entering the US. The 43 additions in August were the largest expansion so far and included electronics and aluminum suppliers.
  • 28 Oct 2026Section 232No new tariffs on robotics parts yet. Section 232 talks come firstOn 28 October the President found that imports of robotics, industrial machinery and their parts threaten national security, then ordered negotiations with trading partners instead of duties. The first progress report is due within 180 days.
  • 26 Oct 2026US–ChinaThe US–China truce runs to 10 January, but two 9 November deadlines still need paperworkThe truce was extended at the September summit. The 178 Section 301 product exclusions and the suspended port fees each need their own USTR notice, and so far only the port fees have one in progress.
  • 13 Aug 2026Drones · FCCDrone tariffs and the FCC Covered List also reach parts that robots useThe FCC now treats foreign-made drone cameras, sensors, batteries, motors, flight controllers and radios as critical components. Separate Section 232 drone duties of up to 100% started on 3 September.
  • 24 Jul 2026Section 301Since 24 July, most imports carry a 10% or 12.5% forced-labor tariffThe Section 301 forced-labor tariffs replaced the expired Section 122 surcharge, which had itself replaced the IEEPA tariffs the Supreme Court struck down. This is what now applies to imported components, and how IEEPA refunds work.

General information as of 29 Oct 2026, compiled from the sources listed. It is not legal, tax or customs advice. Tariff measures change often; confirm the treatment of a specific product with a licensed customs broker or the issuing agency before you rely on it.
